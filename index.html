02 — Clinical Foundations Seattle, then Portland

Clinical Foundations & Early Classrooms

My journey into working with children began in Seattle while attending Shoreline Community College, working as a respite worker for children diagnosed with complex developmental and behavioral needs, including autism, cerebral palsy, oppositional defiance disorder (ODD), and ADHD.

After moving to Portland, I was hired as a toddler room float at Small Wonders School, establishing my initial roots in the local early childhood community.

03 — Global Perspective Away, and back

Global Perspective & Sociological Grounding

As my life expanded through nannying, completing my Associate of Science at Mt. Hood Community College, and traveling to locations like Puerto Vallarta, Mexico—where I explored Yelapa, a small fishing town with cobbled streets and golden beaches, alongside witnessing breaching whales in the ocean—and experiencing ancient Rome from a horse-drawn carriage, the labyrinth of small streets in Venice, and a touch of Florence in Italy, my perspective on the world deepened.

I returned to complete my Bachelor of Science in Anthropology/Sociology with a Concentration in Sociology - Social Welfare at Eastern Oregon University, grounding my educational philosophy in systemic human development and community care.

04 — Mentorship & Curriculum Third year leading kindergarten

Mentorship, Classrooms, and Curriculum

My path at Small Wonders evolved from a toddler float and subbing in the kindergarten studio during travels, to leading preschool rooms, and ultimately stepping into the kindergarten classroom—where I am now in my third year leading the program and was directly mentored by both the previous kindergarten teacher and the school founder.

Here, I found my true calling: teaching children to read, write, and explore art and science deeply.

In my third year, I intentionally restructured our daily schedule to prioritize extended outdoor learning in the Pacific Northwest wonderland alongside focused indoor academics, dynamically building the curriculum as the year progresses to match the exact abilities of the group while maintaining rigorous foundations in literacy and math.

Ms. U’s Kinder Studio

A boutique, intentional micro-school rooted in a Reggio-inspired philosophy where the environment acts as a third teacher. Featuring a tightly capped cohort of eight students on a 4-day model, the studio bridges rigorous early academics with deep, nature-based inquiry.
