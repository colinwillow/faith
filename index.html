About

A teacher at heart, wherever the classroom is

I currently teach preschool and transitional kindergarten at a private school, where my days are full of picture books, paint-covered hands, tricky first friendships, and the thousand tiny breakthroughs that make early childhood education the best job in the world.

My approach is simple: children learn best when they feel known and safe. I build classrooms around play, routine, and genuine relationships — with kids and with their families.

Outside the classroom, I’m always dreaming up what’s next: programs, spaces, and ideas that bring that same warmth to more families.
