01 — About Hi, it's me

A teacher at heart, wherever the classroom is

I teach preschool and transitional kindergarten at a private school, where the days are made of picture books, tricky first friendships, and the thousand tiny breakthroughs that make early childhood the best classroom in the world.

My approach is simple: children learn best when they feel known and safe. So I build rooms around play, routine, and real relationships — with kids, and with their families.

02 — Days Four days a week

A four-day, boutique preschool and kindergarten for eight small humans

  1. Ⅰ

    UFLI-based early literacy

    Explicit, systematic phonics from the UFLI Foundations sequence — sounds, blending, and decoding taught in order, so reading is built rather than guessed at.

  2. Ⅱ

    Nature-based inquiry

    Days that start with a question and go outside to answer it. Mud, magnifying glasses, and seasons watched closely — science before anyone calls it science.

  3. Ⅲ

    An eight-student studio

    Eight children, one teacher, four days a week. Small enough that every child is known well and taught at their own pace — never managed as a crowd.

  4. Ⅳ

    Family partnership

    A class this size means real conversations — conferences, updates, and honest reporting on how your child is actually doing. Parents on the team, always.
