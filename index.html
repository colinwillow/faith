01 — About Hi, it's me

A teacher at heart, wherever the classroom is

I teach preschool and transitional kindergarten at a private school, where the days are made of picture books, tricky first friendships, and the thousand tiny breakthroughs that make early childhood the best classroom in the world.

My approach is simple: children learn best when they feel known and safe. So I build rooms around play, routine, and real relationships — with kids, and with their families.

02 — Days What learning feels like

The everyday work of growing small humans

  1. Ⅰ

    UFLI early literacy

    Explicit phonics, taught in order. Sounds, blending, decoding — reading built, not guessed.

  2. Ⅱ

    Nature-based inquiry

    Outside every day, in every season. A real question first, then mud and magnifying glasses.

  3. Ⅲ

    Social-emotional learning

    Naming big feelings, sharing the good blocks, and practicing what it takes to be a kind friend.

  4. Ⅳ

    Family partnership

    Real conferences and honest updates about your child — not a class average.
