01 — The Program What we teach

Structured where it counts, wide open where it should be

  1. 01

    UFLI early literacy

    The UFLI Foundations sequence, taught explicitly and in order — sounds, blending, decoding. Direct instruction daily, so reading is built rather than guessed at.

    Daily, explicit
  2. 02

    Nature-based inquiry

    Outside every day, in every season. A real question first, then mud, magnifying glasses, and the habits of science long before it’s called science.

    Every day, outdoors
  3. 03

    Social-emotional learning

    Naming big feelings before they boil over, repairing after a hard morning, and building the small community eight children make together.

    Woven through
  4. 04

    Family partnership

    Real conferences and honest updates about your child — not a class average. Eight families is small enough to know every one of them.

    Eight families
